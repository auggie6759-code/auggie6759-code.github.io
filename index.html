ATHENA Personal

A private, single-user voice assistant.

ATHENA Personal is a personal voice assistant built and used by one person, its owner. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and is not available to any other user.

What it does with Gmail

When the owner asks by voice, ATHENA can:

  • search and read the owner's own Gmail messages (gmail.readonly);
  • send an email that the owner has dictated, only after ATHENA reads back the recipient, subject and full text and the owner explicitly confirms (gmail.send).

ATHENA does not delete, archive, label or modify messages, and does not change Gmail settings.
